# Wameedh SC Attendance System — Brief for the Events Department Head

> Plain-language guide to the attendance system: what it does today, what's coming
> for the next event day, and the one list of things to prepare. No technical jargon.

## What is this system

One tool that does three jobs for your events:

1. **Invitations** — it emails every participant their personal invitation with a QR badge.
2. **Check-in** — at the door, the camera reads their QR badge and instantly records them.
3. **The record** — a clean attendance log per session, plus an absent list you can share.

Everything runs locally on the department laptop. Nothing about your participants
or your email password ever leaves that computer.

## The three screens

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Participants** | Your attendee list — import it from a spreadsheet (CSV) or type rows directly. Shows who is registered and who checked in. |
| **QR & Email** | Creates the QR codes for everyone, sends the invitation emails, and keeps a log showing exactly who received theirs. |
| **Scanner** | Turns the camera into a check-in device. A participant shows their badge, the screen flashes "Checked in ✓" in one second. |

## An event day, start to finish

1. **Before the event** — the participants list is ready, one test email is sent, QR codes are generated.
2. **Send invitations** — email set is loaded, preview one email to be sure it looks right, then send to everyone; failed ones can be retried without re-sending to people who already got theirs.
3. **Doors open** — assistants scan as people arrive. Each check-in is stamped with the session, and the same badge can never be counted twice.
4. **Event ends** — one tap pulls the **absent list**, ready to paste straight into a WhatsApp or Telegram message.

## What's coming for the next event day

- **Check-in from anywhere in the room** — assistants can scan with their own phones, so you can run 2-3 stations at the same time instead of one queue at one laptop.
- **No double counting** — scanning the same badge again just says "already checked in at 10:42", nothing gets recorded twice.
- **Sessions tracked automatically** — the Sessions 1-4 you choose on screen are saved with every check-in, so per-session reports come out ready-made.
- **Preview before sending** — see exactly how the email looks before anything goes out.
- **Live counters** — how many invitations went out, how many people arrived, while the event is live.
- **One-tap absent list** — names + emails, copy-ready for the union/board chat.

## The decisions you made (recorded for the record)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Check-in stations | 2-3 devices (main door + assistants' phones) — this arrives first |
| Who presses "Send all" | Assistants can send too — protected by a shared team code, and every send leaves an automatic report of who sent it, to how many people, and the result |
| End-of-event handout | Copy-paste absentee list for messaging chats |
| Event sizes | Some events under 50, Open Day 50-150, biggest ones 150+ — the system will handle the big cohorts in batches |
| On-screen language | English now; French and Arabic come in the polish round |

## What we will need from YOU on event day

These are the only parts that are not automatic:

1. **A Gmail "App Password"** — a one-time procedure in your Google account settings (we will do it together once; it takes 3 minutes). This is not your normal password.
2. **One test email** — confirm the invitation looks right before any real sending.
3. **The participants list** — spreadsheet or ready CSV.
4. **2-3 charged phones** for the extra check-in stations (they open the scanner page from the laptop).
5. **Printed QR badges** for participants who may not have saved the email.

## Still open for you to decide

- Name shown in the email footer next to the club's brand.
- The reply-to address participants answer to.
- What happens when someone loses their badge at the door — we will prepare a fallback where the assistant finds them by name.
